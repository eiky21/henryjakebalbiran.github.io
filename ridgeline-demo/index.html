Questions people ask before booking

Is the survey really free?

Yes. No call-out fee and no obligation to go ahead. You keep the photos either way.

What does "fixed price" mean?

The written quote is what you pay. If we find extra work once the job starts, we cover it, unless you ask us to add something new. client to confirm terms

How soon can you come out?

We agree the survey time with you on the phone. If your roof is leaking now, pick "Urgent" on the form and we put you first.

Which areas do you cover?

Greater Manchester, including Stockport, Trafford, Salford, Tameside and Bury. Put your postcode in the form and we will confirm.

Do I need to be home for the survey?

Not always. For most roofs we only need access to the outside. We will ask when we call.

What happens after I fill in the form?

Our office calls you to book the survey. If you send it outside our hours (Monday to Saturday, 8am to 7pm), we call the next working morning from 8am. Your details are used for this enquiry only.
