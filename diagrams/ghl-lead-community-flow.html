Lead and community flow in GHL

Everything that happens inside GHL once someone fills in a website form, plus the one-off move of the existing community and how events are sold, confirmed and reminded. Draft design, nothing is live yet.

Scroll sideways inside the diagram to see the whole flow.

1 · Website forms

  • One workflow for both forms; every payload key saved to a field
  • Tags: website-, cohort-, consent-
  • Welcome only with ticked consent, never twice

2 · Existing members

  • Imported once as consent-unconfirmed
  • One confirm email; only those who tick consent can be emailed
  • Smart lists update themselves from tags

3 · Events in GHL

  • Invite goes to Community – can email
  • A$50 paid on a GHL booking calendar through Stripe (connection pending)
  • Confirmation, reminders and thank-you are automatic; cancelling stops them
