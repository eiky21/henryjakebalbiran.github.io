The follow-up I'd propose for a clinic

How a request like this could flow through a CRM such as GoHighLevel. Nothing here is connected: no messages are sent from this demo.

  1. Request receivedSaved with the visit type and preferred days; the front desk gets a call task.
  2. Time confirmedOnce staff book a slot in the calendar, the patient gets a confirmation.
  3. Reminder two days beforeA short message with the time and a reschedule link.
  4. Reminder on the morningA final nudge to cut no-shows.

Common questions

Is this a confirmed booking?

No. It's a request. The front desk calls you to agree an exact time before anything is booked.

Can I request for my child?

Yes. Choose "Children's check-up" and mention it when we call.

What if none of my days are free?

We'll offer the closest times we have and you decide.
